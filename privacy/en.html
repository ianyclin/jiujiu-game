9×9 Meteor Squad: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026. Applies to version 1.3 and later (web version, iOS app and Android app).

繁體中文English日本語

In one sentence: 9×9 Meteor Squad collects no personal data at all. There are no accounts, ads, tracking or analytics, and playing does not need the internet. A child's game progress stays on their own device, and we cannot see it. The app's one-time purchase is handled by the App Store / Google Play, and we receive no payment or account details.

1. Who provides this game

9×9 Meteor Squad (九九流星隊) is made and published by 指數三寶飯 (ianyclin). It is a multiplication-table practice game for elementary school children, mainly ages 6 to 11.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send or share names, email addresses, addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, location, photos, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage behavior or crash reports. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or similar device permissions. The Android app includes Google Play's billing library, which brings network-related permissions used only for purchases (see section 4).

3. What is stored on the child's device

So that the game can remember progress, the following is saved in this device's local storage. Only the game on this device can read it. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Answer history (which multiplication facts were right or wrong, and how long they took), high scores and best times;
  • Settings (music, sound, language, which tables to practice);
  • Daily quests, badges, and pet and accessory progress;
  • App only: whether this device has bought the unlock, which games have been tried, and how many times the unlock reminder has appeared (shared by the whole device, not per player);
  • Up to 4 names (nicknames) in "Who's playing". The player types them in, and they are only shown inside the game. Please use a nickname, not a full name.

Backups: a grown-up can use "Back up records" in the "For grown-ups" section of the game's Settings to export these records as a backup file or a backup code, for moving to a new device or from the website to the app. A backup only goes where you choose to keep it (for example, Files or Notes). The game never uploads it or sends it to us. Importing a backup replaces the records on this device.

4. Network and third parties

  • The game's content, images, fonts, music and sounds are all bundled in the app (or served together with the game's web page). While playing, it does not connect to any server, and it works offline (except purchases in the app, see the next item).
  • In-app purchase (app only): Meteor Defense, the Star Map, the pet and daily missions are free. Each of the other four games can be tried once, and a single one-time purchase (not a subscription) unlocks them all. The purchase screen only appears after the parental gate. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The game uses their official purchase functions (StoreKit on iOS, the Google Play Billing Library on Android, called through the open-source Capgo native-purchases plugin, which sends no data to us or any other third party) to ask the store whether the account signed in on this device has bought the unlock. Payment methods, accounts and purchase records are handled by Apple / Google under their own privacy policies. We cannot receive or see them. Google's billing library may send diagnostic information about purchase operations to Google; that is handled by Google.
  • The game contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • Only two links lead out of the game: the author's Facebook page, and this privacy policy. Both ask a grown-up to answer a "parental gate" question first, and open in the system browser. After that, those sites (Facebook, and GitHub Pages where this policy is hosted) handle visit records, such as network addresses, under their own policies. That is outside this game's control.
  • The web version of the game and this policy page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the network addresses of visitors under its own privacy statement. We have not added any tracking code to these pages.

5. Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal data, we do not need to ask parents for consent, and we do not. The game has no social features, chat, user-generated content or ads. The app's only purchase sits behind the parental gate, so children never see a price or a buy button. This approach was designed with the US COPPA, the EU GDPR (including its rules for children), Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information in mind. Since no data is collected, the collection those laws regulate does not take place.

6. Retention and deletion

All data is on the device. We keep no copy, so we cannot look it up or delete it remotely for you. You can do it yourself:

  • In the game, delete a single player under Settings → "For grown-ups" → "Delete player", or open the Star Map page and tap "Erase all records" (a grown-up solves a problem first for both);
  • Uninstall the app, or clear the site data in your browser for the web version. All records disappear with it.

Records do not move to a new device by themselves (use "Back up records", described above, to move them). Also note whole-device backups on iPhone or iPad: if you restore an iCloud or computer backup onto a new device, the system may restore the app's data with it. That is an Apple backup feature; we are not involved and cannot see it. The Android version has app backup turned off.

The app's purchase record is kept by Apple / Google, so deleting the app or erasing records does not affect it. After reinstalling, the game checks with the store automatically, or you can tap "Restore purchase" under Settings → "For grown-ups" → "Unlock all games".

7. Changes to this policy

If a future feature affects privacy (for example, cloud sync), we will update this policy first and design a parental consent step before it ships. The "Last updated" date at the top shows any major change.
